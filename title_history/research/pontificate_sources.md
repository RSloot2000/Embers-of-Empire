# Imperial Pontificate Research

## Scope

This staging set gives EoE title `d_pontificate` a historical holder line for the imperial office of *pontifex maximus*. It begins at the earliest date on which vanilla Augustus can legally hold a CK3 title and ends with Gratian's death on 25 August 383. The same title remains vacant thereafter for EoE's Julianist faith-head gameplay.

The Roman office was personal and collegial practice became increasingly complicated under co-emperors. CK3 permits only one holder, so this file records one representative senior or de facto primary Augustus at a time. It is not a claim that every legitimate co-Augustus lacked pontifical titulature.

## Deliberate Differences From Vanilla Imperial History

- Augustus starts on `3.1.1`, not vanilla `3.1.27`: character 145290 is born on `3.1.1`, so the planned `1.1.1` start would create a holder before birth. Both dates are technical substitutes for his actual accession to the pontificate in 13/12 BC.
- Marcus Aurelius replaces vanilla's Lucius Verus entry at `161.3.7`; Lucius was the junior co-Augustus and cannot share one CK3 title.
- Caracalla replaces vanilla's Geta entry at `211.2.4`; Geta remains a co-Augustus rather than a separate pontificate holder.
- Gordian II and Balbinus are omitted from the single-holder line in 238. Gordian I and Pupienus represent their respective colleges.
- `275.7.1` is a genuine vacancy rather than vanilla's Severina entry. Ulpia Severina's political role is debated, and she could not hold the male priestly office *pontifex maximus*.
- Maximian's one-month vanilla entry in 305 is omitted; Constantius Chlorus begins at the joint abdication on `305.5.1`.
- After Jovian, the line follows the senior western office-holder Valentinian I and then Gratian rather than reproducing vanilla `e_byzantium`'s eastern territorial succession through Valens, Valentinian II, and Theodosius.
- The line ends at `383.8.25`; later Byzantine and western imperial holders in vanilla are not successors to this modeled form of the office.

## Gratian And The End Of The Office

Gratian is traditionally described as the first emperor to refuse the pontifical robe. Zosimus associates the refusal with a visit to Rome sometimes dated to 376, but both the visit and the dating have been questioned. Modern discussion places the break broadly between 376 and the anti-pagan measures of 382/383. The model retains Gratian until his death on 25 August 383 because that date is unambiguous and prevents false precision.

The title does not pass to Valentinian II, Magnus Maximus, or Theodosius I. Late imperial titulature shifted toward *pontifex inclytus*, while the old imperial use of *pontifex maximus* ceased. EoE can therefore reuse the vacant `d_pontificate` as the religious head of a restored Julianist faith without extending the ancient holder line into playable bookmarks.

## EoE Mechanics Boundary

EoE defines `d_pontificate` as landless and binds it to `julianism`. Its landed-title definition currently uses `destroy_if_invalid_heir`, `no_automatic_claims`, and `always_follows_primary_heir`. This historical staging does not change those fields. A separate in-game test must determine whether they correctly model a restored spiritual or appointed faith head.

The existing title localization and coat of arms are reused. No custom characters, dynasties, nicknames, mottos, traits, events, decisions, or title mechanics are added.
