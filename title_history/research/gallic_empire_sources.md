# Gallic Empire Research

## Scope

This staging set identifies EoE title `e_gaul` with the Gallic Empire of AD 260-274. It records the de facto imperial line from Postumus through Tetricus and ends when Aurelian reincorporated the territory. The Domain of Soissons is a later and distinct polity and is excluded.

`k_gallia` and `k_septem_provinciae` represent Roman dioceses, not sovereign kingdoms. They receive no parallel holder history. Their vicars may be suitable for a later appointed administrative system, or for republican or theocratic title forms where the office and CK3 mechanics warrant it, but they must not be presented as hereditary kings.

## Date Conventions

- `260.9.1`: conventional accession for Postumus after the Rhine crisis and death of Saloninus.
- `269.2.1`: conventional start of Laelianus's rival claim.
- `269.5.1`: conventional end of Laelianus's revolt.
- `269.6.1`: conventional transition from Postumus to Marius.
- `269.9.1`: conventional transition from Marius to Victorinus after an approximately three-month reign.
- `271.3.1`: conventional transition from Victorinus to Tetricus and start of Domitianus's rival claim.
- `273.1.1`: conventional date for Tetricus Iunior's elevation as Caesar.
- `274.3.1`: conventional end of the Gallic Empire during Aurelian's reconquest.

These dates create a coherent CK3 sequence and must not be read as exact ancient calendar dates.

## Names And Dynasties

Localized names use attested Latin forms and retain ASCII-safe script keys. Common modern search forms include Postumus, Laelianus or Laelian, Marius, Victorinus, Victoria or Vitruvia, Domitian II, Tetricus I, and Tetricus II.

The Cassianii, Laeliani, Marii, Piavonii, Domitianus and Esuvii definitions are CK3 fallback identities based on attested name elements. They do not prove broad ancient pedigrees. Only Tetricus and his son are securely linked. Victoria and Victorinus share Piavonian identity solely to support the reported mother-son relationship; no relationship between Victoria and Tetricus is encoded.

All characters use EoE's `roman` culture and `hellenic_pagan` faith. This represents their Roman imperial identity and the traditional cults visible in third-century imperial coinage; it does not claim uniform personal theology.

## Explicit Exclusions

- Postumus Junior and Victorinus Junior: Historia Augusta-only figures without adequate numismatic support.
- A wife or additional sons for Postumus: debated portrait interpretations cannot establish named relatives.
- Flavian descent for Domitianus: late literary construction, not demonstrated ancestry.
- A Victoria-Tetricus family link: proposed but unsupported.
- Victoria as empress or title holder: political influence is not independent imperial tenure.
- Tetricus Iunior as co-holder: Caesar and dynastic heir, not a securely independent Augustus in the selected line.
- Soissons, diocesan vicars and praetorian prefects: separate historical and mechanical projects.
